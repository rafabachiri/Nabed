What is Nabed?

The problem it solves and who it serves.

Nabed (“pulse” in Arabic) helps medical students bridge the gap from French to English medical terminology — the language of international research, journals and practice.

Most quality medical-English tools are paid, generic, and not aligned with the Algerian curriculum. Nabed is a free, curriculum-aligned, fully bilingual alternative that turns vocabulary mastery into a daily habit through game mechanics, spaced repetition and teacher-guided learning.

The student experience

A guided daily loop: learn → practise → play → track.

Personal dashboard

On sign-in, students land on a personalised home screen showing their streak, level and XP, a daily challenge, quick stats (words learned, weekly XP, duels won), recommended modules, a practice hub and a peek at the weekly leaderboard.

Core learning tools

  • Learn: browse vocabulary by body system & year; each module shows progress and ends with a 10-question quiz.
  • Advanced English: rich topic articles (e.g. cardiovascular disorders, clinical pharmacology, critical care) with key bilingual terms.
  • Flashcards: a spaced-repetition (SM-2) engine surfaces due cards and adapts to each learner's memory.
  • Translator: instant FR↔EN search with definitions, examples, phonetics, word roots and text-to-speech audio.
  • Practice: pronunciation (speech recognition), listening (audio MCQ) and writing exercises.
  • Clinical cases: bilingual scenarios that build comprehension and contextual vocabulary.

Content & personalisation

  • Videos & Courses: watch teaching videos and follow teacher-created courses.
  • Favorites: bookmark any word, video or course and find them all in one tabbed hub.
  • Profile: level, XP progress, streak, words learned, earned badges and saved words.
Designed for habit. Every action earns XP, every day extends a streak, and the spaced-repetition system keeps bringing learners back at exactly the right moment — turning study into a game they want to win.

Gamification & motivation

The mechanics that keep students engaged and coming back.

⭐

XP & Levels

Every quiz, game, flashcard and exercise awards XP. XP unlocks levels from “Student” all the way to “Professor”.

🔥

Daily Streaks

Consecutive active days build a streak — a powerful nudge to return every day.

🏅

Badges

Achievements unlock automatically from real progress (first word, 7-day streak, 100 words, perfect quiz…).

🏆

Leaderboards

Compete nationally or filter by your own university to defend your cohort's honour.

The five medical games

GameWhat you doReward
Term BlastPick the correct English translation against the clock+8 XP / answer
AnagramReorder scrambled letters into the medical term+10 XP / answer
Medical RootsIdentify the root, prefix or suffix of a term+12 XP / answer
Diagnostic MatchMatch terms to their English definitions+15 XP / pair
The Duel SOONReal-time head-to-head against another student+50 XP / win

Level progression

Levels carry meaningful medical titles that reflect a real career path — Student → Apprentice → Intern → Resident → Practitioner → Specialist → Physician → Head of Clinic → Professor — giving long-term goals beyond a single session.

How it works

From sign-up to mastery in three simple steps.

Create an account

Free and instant. Students choose their study year and priority systems; teachers submit a request for approval.

Learn with flashcards & modules

The spaced-repetition engine plans reviews while modules, the translator and practice build breadth.

Play, compete & climb

Earn XP through games and exercises, keep a daily streak, unlock badges and rise up the leaderboard.

Technology & reliability

Nabed is built on a modern, proven stack designed for speed, security and easy growth.
